# Kennedy Space Center

The John F. Kennedy Space Center is NASA's launch complex on Merritt Island, Florida. Established in 1962 and named after President Kennedy in 1963, it was the launch site of every crewed Apollo mission. Launch Complex 39, with pads 39A and 39B, was built for the Saturn V; Apollo 11 lifted off from pad 39A on 16 July 1969. The center later launched Skylab and the Space Shuttle, and its Vehicle Assembly Building was where Saturn V rockets were stacked.
